Now try the real thing

Tap these and note the exact wording of the error Safari shows. It points at the cause:

  • www.gov.uk
  • CRM site
  • "Server unexpectedly dropped the connection" – something on the network is cutting the connection (firewall, filter, router, or carrier).
  • "A TLS error caused the secure connection to fail" – HTTPS is being interfered with, or the phone's clock is wrong.
  • "Server cannot be found" / "cannot find the server" – DNS problem.
  • "Request timed out" – packets being dropped (IPv6 route, MTU, or firewall).
  • "Blocked" / "Restricted" – a content filter or Screen Time.

Settings worth checking on the phone

  • Settings > General > VPN & Device Management: any profile listed (remove or note it).
  • Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > Web Content.
  • Settings > Wi-Fi > (i) next to the network: Limit IP Address Tracking off, Private Wi-Fi Address off, Configure DNS = Automatic.
  • Settings > Apps > Safari > Advanced: Advanced Tracking and Fingerprinting Protection set to Off, and Experimental Features reset to defaults.
  • Settings > Privacy & Security: Lockdown Mode off.
  • Settings > General > Date & Time: Set Automatically on.
  • Settings > Mobile Service: turn Wi-Fi Assist off while testing.
  • Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Reset > Reset Network Settings.
